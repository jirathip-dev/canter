# impl-202 — freeze a reviewed delivery, and bind p5's certified head (Refs #202)

Candidate commit: `a6d4b33fcbeed5d0921dcc85e18bd6db8201313e` (this report commit is
its child; `git status --porcelain` is empty at both).
Base: published `origin/staging` = `2d3d1ef8c65102fa1fd2e3c0fa1e1df9997182de`
(verified with `git ls-remote origin refs/heads/staging` before any edit).

## What the two defects were (read-only reproduction at the pinned base)

- **(a) nothing freezes a delivery after review.** The engine could only
  *observe* movement: the merge read the delivery branch's head and landed
  whatever it found (`feature_tree = rev-parse <branch>^{tree}`, `src/mutation.rs`),
  while the evidence gate proved a verdict for the *certified* head. A commit
  pushed by the lane worker after the verdict was therefore consumed silently
  under the old verdict. Reproduced by the new witness's mutation: with the
  freeze removed the merge reports `mode: landed`, `reconciled: true`,
  `landed_head` = the post-verdict commit.
- **(b) `p5` could bind a head no collection observed.** The run's
  `feature_head` was scraped from ANY successful apply response's `head` key
  (`state::run_dispatch_context`), and `collect_outcome`'s outcome carried no
  *typed* binding of its own. Measured on the live recording for
  `run-f91ece4defffcced`: `ik_...-p2-132-1789744135` (a `worktree_create`
  response) bound `2d3d1ef` — the BASE — as the run's feature head before any
  collection had certified anything; a run whose collection never succeeded
  still presented that head to its later steps. Reproduced by the new
  witness's mutation: the run's `feature_head` becomes the echoed base again.

Design choices, stated plainly:

- The engine cannot stop an external `git push` by the pane worker, so AC1 is
  enforced where consumption happens: **the consumed head must be exactly the
  head the recorded verdict names**. When the published integration ref has not
  moved, the merge consumes the delivery branch only at that head; a
  post-verdict commit refuses `refusal.delivery.moved` (naming both heads) and
  the delivery must re-enter review. The refusal is a recorded typed outcome
  (#200's discipline): the frontier parks with the retries unspent.
- `#178`'s published-ref reconciliation is deliberately untouched: it is the one
  documented *engine* refresh (a history rewrite onto the **published** ref
  whose reviewed content is proven byte-for-byte) and re-certifies in its own
  bounded retry. The freeze rule applies to the branch the *worker* moves, not
  to that proven reconcile path — the #178 witness
  (`cycle2_merge_reconciles_then_lands_a_delivery_behind_the_moved_published_ref`)
  still passes unchanged.
- No deadline, freshness bound or retry budget was widened; nothing was added to
  a suppression/allow list; no dependency changed.

## AC-by-AC disposition

- **AC1 (freeze / re-entry)** — enforced: the consumed delivery head must be the
  verdict's head (`refusal.delivery.moved` otherwise, with both heads named);
  the diagnosis parks typed with retries unspent and names the re-entry
  requirement. Witnessed RED/GREEN by mutation 1. Scope stated honestly: the
  engine refuses to *consume* a moved delivery; it does not (and cannot) block
  the external push itself. The automated re-collect-and-re-review loop (a fresh
  `collect_outcome` re-binding a moved head, then a new review round) is NOT
  implemented — that is the re-entry an operator/next run must perform, and
  `refusal.delivery.moved` is the typed outcome that demands it (never a silent
  park).
- **AC2 (p5 binds its observed head)** — the collector binds typed or refuses
  (`refusal.collect.unbound`); the binding is recorded and attributed
  (`run_delivery_certificate`); the run's `feature_head` comes only from the
  collection or an explicit `feature_head` response; and a `review_evidence`/
  `merge` step consuming any other head refuses `refusal.delivery.unbound`
  before any effect. Witnesses: mutation 2 (state) + mutation 3 (product
  surface). The one shape I could not reach with the real `git` binary is a
  collection effect whose `rev-parse --verify HEAD` prints a non-sha (real git
  either prints a 40-hex sha or exits non-zero), so `certify_delivery_binding`
  is witnessed by a pure unit test plus the state-level binding witness rather
  than by an end-to-end effect run.
- **AC3 (zero operator presentation)** — not proved end-to-end here, and I am
  not claiming it. What this lane adds is the *precondition*: the head the run's
  own collection observed is bound and consumed by the review/merge without any
  operator input, and the two new codes are typed refusals (never
  `waiting-approval`). The driver-only half of the spine remains witnessed by
  the existing base tests (`queue_advance::the_supervisor_itself_drives_the_committed_merge_and_cleanup_to_the_last_step`,
  `host_proof_renewal::a_submit_time_proof_renews_at_dispatch_time_and_the_fanout_steps_are_reached`,
  both still green in the suites above); a single p1..p8 zero-operator run
  needs a fixture whose merge has a real published ref and a self-dispatching
  review leg, which this lane did not build.
- **AC4 (witnesses that bite)** — table above.
- **AC5 (non-weakening)** — `#195`/`#193` review self-dispatch, `#196` publish,
  `#199` host-proof renewal, `#200` settled-turn certification and
  `#192` frontier carry-forward suites are untouched and green:
  `host_proof_renewal`, `review_dispatch`, `frontier_carry_forward`,
  `supervision`, `queue_advance`, `mutation_engine` all pass in the raw runs
  above (`just ci` runs the whole matrix). No deadline/freshness/retry bound was
  widened; the only new classification is the impossible-step park, which
  *spends fewer* retries, not more.
