## 2. The policy the issue asks to be stated (required-fix item 2)

**Policy 1 — the cleanup step's own bounded wait — is the delivered policy.**
Policy 2 ("the publish step retires the run's own lane after a successful
publish") is **not** implemented. Why: the cleanup effect's landing proof runs
BEFORE its workspace retirement, so a lane still alive at that point has
already had its delivery certified — the only thing left is timing, and the
wait absorbs exactly that. The wait preserves every protection item 3 fences
off: the close keeps its own whole-lane verification (lane token, generation,
worktree, settled agent state), a lane that started working again voids the
confirmation (`refusal.lane.busy`), a superseded generation or a foreign
workspace refuses at once, and a bound that expires parks
`effect.lane_timeout` with the workspace, checkout and branch untouched and the
bounded retries unspent. Retiring the run's own lane at publish time instead
would close the workspace of a worker that is demonstrably still iterating —
the measured case the issue itself describes — i.e. it would retire a LIVE
lane, which item 3 forbids. Policy 1 is also the only one of the two that
leaves the live proof to the run's own p8, with no new state-machine step.
