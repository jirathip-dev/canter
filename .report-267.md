## 1. What the issue asked, and what this slice lands

Issue #267 (conductor spec; the residual of #203) asked for four things:

1. **The plan declares the skills per leg** — which procedure a lane is given
   becomes part of the plan and therefore of the plan digest, not an accident
   of a worker's profile contents. Worker lanes stay canter-agnostic.
2. **Role→skill bindings are configuration**, recorded in the plan digest —
   never hardcoded in code or prompt text; a plan naming an unresolvable skill
   refuses typed instead of starting a lane short of its procedure.
3. **Three role contracts as installable procedure sources** (implementer,
   reviewer, orchestrator — the last one new).
4. **`run status` shows, per leg, the role and the skills the lane was given**
   (the board is a disclosed gap, section 4).

Landed surfaces:

- `hf-config/v1` gains the role-skills axis: `harness.<key>.skills` (the role
  skills one role binding gives its lane) and the `skill.<key>` inventory
  (each entry the 64-hex content identity of one installed procedure). A
  binding that declares a key the inventory does not resolve refuses the whole
  binding typed (`refusal.skill.unresolved`, exit 4) — fail-closed, never a
  partial set, never a silent skip.
- `hf-profile-binding/v1` material gains `skills` (resolved `{key, hash}`
  pins), so the binding revision — and therefore every plan digest that binds
  the binding — fingerprints the procedure.
- The bound-input plan document (`hf-queue-preview/v1`) gains `legs`: one
  `{issue, role, round, agent, workspace, checkout, skills}` entry per
  declared leg, deterministically derived from the role bindings and the step
  spine and re-derived by `queue.submit` (a document whose legs disagree with
  its bindings moves the digest and refuses). The implementer leg's resolved
  pins ride `role_config.skills`; the reviewer leg's ride the review step's
  existing `reviewer_profile`. The preview render also carries `steps[].skills`
  (render-only) beside the per-step `lane` object.
- `run status` renders the run's committed plan legs (`legs: [{role, skills}]`)
  from the durable bound-input line (`State::run_plan_legs` +
  `state::plan_legs_doc`).
- Three committed role procedure sources: `skills/lane-implementer/SKILL.md`,
  `skills/lane-reviewer/SKILL.md`, `skills/lane-orchestrator/SKILL.md` (plus a
  pointer from `skills/canter/SKILL.md`).
- `canter config show` reports, per harness row, the declared skills with the
  identity each resolves to (`hash: null` marks an unresolved declaration).

  own slice rather than a rider on this one. The read path it needs exists and
  is tested here (`State::run_plan_legs` + `state::plan_legs_doc`, witnessed
