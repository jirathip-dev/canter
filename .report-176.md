# Issue #176 implementation lane — the p7 merge honours the plan's squash policy and publishes the certified delivery

Refs #176, #132, #156, #178, #182, #190. Candidate commits: `06bcae8` (the
landing) and `90357ee` (never publish to a production integration branch);
this report is the following commit and cannot cite its own head — the pushed
head sha is in the lane's final message and `git ls-remote origin impl-176`.

## The defect

The committed tail of the acceptance spine declares `merge_policy:"squash"`
(the plan's own policy) while `effect_merge` was a read-only REHEARSAL: it
verified the reviewed tree and the published ref, then recorded
`mode:"rehearsal", landed:false` and returned **success**. On
`run-03037c468003cd8b` (issue #132) every step through `p7-132` "succeeded",
yet `origin/staging` was unchanged, the integration clone was unchanged, the
delivery head was on no published ref, and `p8-132` refused
`refusal.cleanup.unmerged` four times — the run could never finish. A step
that reports success while publishing nothing is a false success, and the
tail was unreachable by construction.

## What was delivered

- `src/mutation.rs` — `effect_merge` now LANDS the certified delivery under
  the plan's closed policy and PUBLISHES it to the integration remote, a
  control-plane mutation the daemon journals (`mutate.merge` +
  `outcome.mutate.merge`) like every other effect:
  - `squash` writes ONE integration commit whose tree is the delivered tree
    and whose parent is the published head (`git commit-tree`, the daemon's
    own deterministic identity); `ff` lands the delivered head itself.
  - the landing is fast-forwarded into the integration checkout (`git merge
    --ff-only`, never a rewrite, never a forced update) and pushed to the
    same `origin` remote the merge target was read from
    (`git push --porcelain <sha>:refs/heads/<integration>`, never force); the
    published ref is then read back with `git ls-remote`, and the step
    succeeds only when it carries the landing.
  - a landing that cannot be published records a typed non-success: a
    checkout that cannot advance refuses `effect.merge.failed` BEFORE
    anything is published (uncommitted operator work is never discarded),
    and a publish that fails refuses `effect.merge.failed` — or the bounded
    `refusal.run.retry_required` when the published ref moved — with the
    integration checkout rolled back to the published head (`git reset
    --keep`), so no local move the published ref does not carry is ever left
    behind (issue #156's unpublished-local-move state is never manufactured).
  - a delivery whose content is already on the merge target (a prior landing
    of this very delivery, or a delivery with no content beyond the base)
    reports `mode:"already-landed"` and publishes nothing.
  - the outcome now records `published_head`, `published_after`,
    `landed_head` (the head the published ref carries), the pre-landing
    `checkout_head`, `certified_head`, `reconciled_head`, `reconciled`,
    `result_tree` and `mode` (`landed` | `already-landed`); `landed` is
    `true` on every success.
- `src/mutation.rs` — `merge_inputs` refuses a topology whose integration
  branch is a production branch (declared, or the hardcoded `main`) with
  `refusal.policy.push`, in the step's own param contract: the landing
  publishes to the integration ref, and production promotion stays a
  separate human-gated path.
- `src/mutation.rs` — `certified_content_differences` factors the fail-closed
  content fact (reviewed paths, NUL-delimited names, literal pathspecs, both
  rename endpoints) that the squash landing, the reconciliation and the
  cleanup landed proof share; `prove_certified_content` keeps its exact
  message. The `!feature_descends` squash case (a delivery that does not
  contain the merge target) now proves its reviewed content is already on
  the target and reports `already-landed`, or refuses — it never rewrites the
  target's own content.
- Unchanged and re-witnessed: the #156 published-ref merge target (never the
  checkout's stale local view), the #182 bounded reconciliation loop
  (`refusal.run.retry_required`, then re-certify), the daemon-side evidence
  gate, the closed `squash | ff` policy, `revalidate_effect`, the
  capability/epoch checks, `refusal.cleanup.unmerged` and the ancestry-only
  standalone `branch_delete`. No cap, gate, budget or refusal was widened or
  weakened; nothing was hand-merged; the live acceptance sandbox and the real
  forge were never touched (fixtures only).
- Docs kept truthful: `docs/contracts/spec-plans.md` (the merge contract),
  `docs/contracts/spec-daemon.md` (committed-tail dispatch), the
  `COMMITTED_TAIL_STEP_KINDS` doc comment in `src/supervision.rs`, the
  `LandedProof`/`MergeInputs`/`published_integration_head` doc comments, and
  a `CHANGELOG.md` entry.
