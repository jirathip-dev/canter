## The claim under test — both halves hold at this head

**Half 1 — #139 starts the Herdr pane worker inside the run's lane worktree.**
Measured path: the daemon's apply (`method_apply` → `method_apply_from`,
`src/daemon.rs`) calls `crate::mutation::execute_step` (the one call site
every applied step goes through, `src/daemon.rs:2983`) → `effect_harness_start`
(`src/mutation.rs:2700`) → `run_lane_worktree` resolves the single lane
worktree from the reviewed plan (and refuses when the plan cannot name
exactly one) → `execute_op_in_worktree(…, cwd = lane worktree)` → `herdr_start`
→ `herdr_lane::start_with_env` (`src/adapters/herdr_lane.rs:178`):
`herdr worktree open --cwd <repo root> --path <lane worktree> --label <lane>
--no-focus` (`herdr_lane.rs:264-275`), and the read-backs are verified — the
workspace's `checkout_path` and the single pane's `cwd` must be the lane
worktree (`herdr_lane.rs:282-297`), and every substrate row runs with the
lane worktree as its process cwd.

**Half 2 — `collect_outcome` stays fail-closed on `empty_delta`.**
Measured path: `effect_collect_outcome` (`src/mutation.rs:3223`) →
`await_pane_worker` (`:3109`) → `poll_pane_worker` (`:3165`): a turn settles
only when the SAME stop state is read back twice; a non-succeeded outcome
that is not `refusal.collect.empty_delta` returns at once; an empty delta with
a live worker keeps WAITING, bounded by the step deadline, and parks as
`effect.worker_timeout` (`ambiguous`, no head, no redispatch). With the stop
confirmed and still no delta, `collect_worktree_outcome` refuses
`refusal.collect.empty_delta` (`:3322`) when `requires_delta` is declared —
never a certified head, never a silent carry-on. The built-in selected-issue
spine declares `requires_delta: true` on its prompt and collection steps
(`src/plan.rs:274`, `:284`); the documented `requires_delta:false` no-op
exception (`docs/contracts/spec-plans.md:48-50`) is unchanged and out of this
claim's shape.
