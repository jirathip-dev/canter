## 2. The change (one behaviour, bounded)

What the delivered wait did: it polled `close_lane_workspace` every 100 ms and
closed the lane's workspace on the FIRST read-back reporting `idle`/`done`.

What the collection (issue #170 N7) requires before it certifies anything: a
CONFIRMED stop — `COLLECT_STOP_SAMPLES` consecutive corroborated read-backs,
both views non-working, the lane's own lifecycle counter unmoved, spanning the
real `COLLECT_STOP_INTERVAL_SECS` interval. Its recorded rationale is the
measured flap: a status that reports `idle`/`done` MID-TURN. A one-read settle
is strictly weaker than the discipline the issue names, and closing on it can
retire the workspace of a lane that is still alive — the protection issue #224
fences off.

Delivered:

- `src/mutation.rs` — the cleanup wait samples the lane through the
  collection's own read-back (`adapters::observe_pane_worker`: the lane's own
  row, its status row, its lifecycle counter), confirms the settled turn with
  the collection's constants and rule, and only then calls the same whole-lane
  `close_lane_workspace` verification it always called. A sample reporting the
  lane still working never reaches a close; a busy close voids the
  confirmation and the wait re-confirms; a lane whose own read-back cannot be
  taken at all falls straight through to the close (nothing observable is not
  waited on); every refusal that is not the timing condition — above all a
  superseded generation or another lane's workspace — still returns at once.
  The bound (the step's effective deadline), the `effect.lane_timeout` park
  (workspace preserved, bounded retries unspent) and the recorded `lane_wait`
  are unchanged. The loop is not `poll_pane_worker`'s: that contract treats a
  non-empty failure as actionable and returns it (which the collection needs),
  while this wait must keep waiting on `refusal.lane.busy`; the FACTS it shares
  (the sample count, the interval, the corroboration rule) are reused, not
  copied as new constants.
- `src/mutation.rs` unit tests (`cleanup_lane_wait_*`, clock-seamed like the
  collection's own): a flap is never a settle; the close happens only at the
  confirmation; a moving lifecycle counter never settles; a busy close
  re-confirms; a typed refusal is never waited on; an unobservable lane is not
  waited on.
- `tests/herdr_pane_execution.rs` — the fake substrate's two views of the lane
  now report the SAME state (one lane flapping, one state) and a new
  `HF_FAKE_HERDR_FLAP_UNTIL_LIST_READS` control models the measured flap. The
  new witness
  `p8_never_closes_a_flapping_live_lane_and_closes_it_after_the_settle`
  asserts: (a) a flap that outlives the step's bound parks `effect.lane_timeout`
  with the workspace, checkout and pane PRESERVED, ZERO `workspace close` rows
  and no close ATTEMPT at all (the workspace is never even probed — asserted
  on the substrate's own row log); (b) once the flap ends, only the CONFIRMED
  settle closes it, exactly once, and the recorded wait cost its real
  intervals. The existing #224 witness keeps its assertions; its settle leg now
  costs the confirmation intervals, so its step bound is sized for a loaded
  host (30 s -> 180 s, both bound assertions updated).
- Canonical contracts updated where they own the wait
  (`docs/contracts/spec-daemon.md`, `spec-lifecycle.md`,
  `spec-capabilities.md`) plus one CHANGELOG entry under Unreleased.
