# Issue #178 implementation lane — the p7 merge now reconciles a certified delivery onto the moved published ref

Refs #178, #156, #132. Candidate commit: `dfaf58e3556262da867f8988024aac56609483c4`
(this report is the following commit and cannot cite its own head).

## What was delivered

The defect: `p7-<issue>` (the committed merge) refused terminally whenever the
published integration ref moved while the run was in flight, so a certified
delivery could never merge and the run's committed tail (`p7` + `p8`) was
unreachable on any busy repository.

- `src/mutation.rs` — `effect_merge` now records the FETCHED published head as
  its certifiable merge target. When the checkout is strictly BEHIND the
  published ref, the published ref is fetched into the checkout's
  `refs/remotes/origin/<integration>` and verified to equal the `ls-remote`
  read. A certified delivery that does not contain the published head has its
  own delivered commits replayed onto it (`git rebase --onto`) in the
  delivery's contained lane worktree, the certified content is proven
  byte-identical on every path the review covered, and the step then reports
  the bounded `refusal.run.retry_required` naming the reconciled head. The
  next attempt (the existing bounded retry) re-certifies the reconciled head
  against the fetched published ref. The outcome records `published_head`,
  `checkout_head`, `certified_head`, `reconciled_head` and `reconciled`;
  `landed` stays `false` and no integration ref, checkout or remote is moved.
  A published ref that moves AGAIN between those attempts is reconciled again
  (the same bounded loop); each attempt consumes the shared budget.
- `src/mutation.rs` — `effect_post_merge_verify` proves containment by exact
  ancestry (unchanged) or, for a squash landing that rewrites the reviewed
  commits and can therefore never be an ancestor, by the same fail-closed
  content fact the cleanup landed proof uses: every path the reviewed head
  changed relative to the reviewed base must carry the reviewed head's exact
  content in the integration ref. Plan (`merge_policy:"squash"`), merge
  rehearsal and verifier now agree on the squash landing.
- `tests/mutation_engine.rs` — witness (a): a certified delivery behind the
  moved published ref reconciles, re-certifies, reaches a merged integration
  head through the squash landing, passes `post_merge_verify` (content route)
  and closes the tail (`i1` close + `x1` cleanup with `landed_by: content`).
  Witness (b): the unpublished local move (checkout AHEAD of the published
  ref) still refuses `effect.merge.not_fast_forward` and never rewrites the
  delivery; the absent-ref and unreadable-`origin` refusals are unchanged.
- `docs/contracts/spec-plans.md` — the published merge-step contract describes
  the behind-the-published-ref reconciliation, its fail-closed boundaries and
  the verifier's content route.

Fail-closed boundaries kept: the published ref is still read only via
`ls-remote` (never the checkout's own refs); an absent or unreadable published
ref still refuses `effect.merge.failed`; an unpublished or diverged local view
still refuses `effect.merge.not_fast_forward` and is never reconciled; a fetch
that cannot run, a fetched head that disagrees with the published read, a
missing/foreign/dirty lane worktree, a rebase conflict (aborted before the
refusal) and any content divergence all refuse with diagnostics.
`INSTANCE_STATE`, caps and grants are untouched; no direct DB mutation; no gate
bypass; no hand-merging.

## Semantic choice and residual tension (explicit)

The plan authors `p7` with `merge_policy:"squash"`. Honouring squash while
keeping `post_merge_verify`'s exact-ancestry proof is impossible, because a
squash landing rewrites the reviewed commits. This change therefore makes the
squash landing the real semantic end-to-end: the reconciliation replays the
certified delta onto the published ref (linear, landable by the repository's
squash merge), and the verifier proves the reviewed content is present
byte-identically when ancestry cannot hold. That is the choice the brief's
direction implies ("the three must agree"); the residual tension is that the
verifier's squash-landing proof is content-equivalence rather than exact
ancestry — the same fail-closed fact the accepted cleanup landed proof uses
(Refs #132, #172), with NUL-delimited names, literal pathspecs and both rename
endpoints. The ancestry route remains first and unchanged for every ff/merge
landing.

Two further disclosures:

1. The reconciliation rewrites the local delivery branch; the remote lane
   branch (the PR head) is not force-pushed by this change. The engine has no
   force-push path, and the forge's own 3-way squash of the released head
   produces the same content this proof covers. What the step records is the
   published-ref certification of the reconciled head.
2. Witness (a) exercises the bounded retry as the step's own fresh bounded
   re-dispatch after the `refusal.run.retry_required` outcome. The armed
   supervision driver's automatic consumption of the shared retry budget
   (Refs #179) is unchanged by this lane and is witnessed in
   `tests/supervision.rs`; the queue-run retry fence (`claim_run_retry`) is
   not exercised by these fixtures.
