  the header note.

  *effect's* own apply claim/journal stays exactly as it was (that is AC3's
  "existing cleanup journaling preserved"); the pass stall is reported on the
  surface (`daemon status` / `supervision status`) and clears when the effect

  the age is what separates a slow lane call from a wedge). A `prompt` step's

  wait and re-evaluates at its next cadence, and the run's in-flight claim
  fences any re-dispatch.

- The new suite's runtime is ~12–16 s standalone; it is its own 150 s-budget
  matrix cell.
