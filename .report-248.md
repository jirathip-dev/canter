## Policy implemented (issue's required fix 1) and why

**The plan is RE-BOUND for the round before the step is dispatched**, performed
by the ONE control authorized to advance the round (the bounded re-evaluation
control, `run.reevaluate`). Rejected alternative: "the step derives its checkout
from the leg's own current round". That policy would make the plain dispatch of
a plan bound to a previous round PROCEED by silently reinterpreting another
leg's checkout, which AC2 requires to refuse typed and AC3 requires to keep
refusing for a genuinely foreign checkout. The re-binding is therefore an
explicit, bounded, attributed act of the control (the only actor that advances
the round), and the invariant check in the effect is untouched.

- `src/mutation.rs`: `rebound_reviewer_lane(issue, params, round)` derives the
  reviewer leg's OWN checkout (`issues-<N>-rev<R>`, `crate::lane`) for the round
  being dispatched, and returns `None` unless the committed binding is a
  byte-exact checkout of THIS issue's reviewer leg for another round (a foreign
  checkout — the run's own lane, a sibling leg's, another issue's, a
  non-canonical round token — is never reinterpreted), and unless the substrate
  is the pane substrate (the bare-subprocess fallback keeps its binding byte for
  byte, per the plan's own rule).
- `src/daemon.rs`: `run.reevaluate` merges that derived checkout over the
  committed params, exactly as it already merges the derived lane round; the
  control still presents no check status, no verdict and no head. ONE state read
  of the committed step documents replaces the two the gate already made.
- Required fix 4 (the fix round's own dispatch binds the round it dispatches):
  #238's fix leg already derives its lane from the round it dispatches
  (`lane_checkout(issue, "implementer", leg.round + round)`); this change
  applies the same discipline to the leg the fix round advanced — the reviewer
  leg of the re-review — which is where the stale binding was actually created
  (the plan is immutable and digest-bound; the binding is presented per
  dispatch). Item 2 (typed refusal + a supervised path that performs the
  re-render): the refusal is unchanged and now the supervised path performs the
  re-render instead of parking (`run.reevaluate` is exactly the control the
  parked run's `supervision.step_diagnosed` names).
- Contracts: `docs/contracts/spec-daemon.md` (the control's own paragraph),
  `CHANGELOG.md` (`### Fixed`).
